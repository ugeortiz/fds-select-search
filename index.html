Products — search and filter while items are selected

LPD-109703 — the search and the filters no longer disappear when you select items. Select a few products, search or filter for something else and keep selecting: the selection bar tells you how many are selected and how many of them you can't see right now. "Show selected" lists the whole selection on its own, and Delete warns you before touching items that aren't on screen.
